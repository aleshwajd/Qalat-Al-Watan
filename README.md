# 🇦🇪 Qalat Al-Watan (قلعة الوطن)

**Qalat Al-Watan** is a multi-genre mobile game concept and interactive web prototype deeply anchored in Emirati heritage, blending sports simulation, AI-driven roleplay, and creative storytelling with a real-world rewards economy.

## 🌟 Core Game Modes

1. **⚽ Al-Saheleya Street Football:** Fast-paced 5v5 arcade football set across iconic UAE backdrops (Corniche, Oases, and Heritage Yards) with custom kit design and team management.
2. **🏛️ Grand Council of Eras (Majlis Al-Zaman):** A high-stakes historical strategy game where players answer cultural dilemmas judged dynamically by AI elders evaluating *Hikma* (Wisdom), *Balagha* (Eloquence), and *Mintaq* (Logic).
3. **📖 Al-Freej Interactive Stories (Riwayat Al-Freej):** Episodic folklore adventures where players read local tales and write their own continuations, evaluated in real-time by an AI creative engine.

## 🎁 Real-Life Rewards (*Souq al-Jawa'iz*)
Earn Dirham Points (DP) through gameplay and redeem them for real-world lifestyle vouchers, theme park passes, cultural attraction tickets, and local dining perks across the UAE.

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic
* **Visuals:** Glassmorphism UI framed with traditional *Al-Sadu* geometric patterns and mashrabiya lattice work.
* **Palette:** Deep night navy, desert gold, palm-frond green, and pearl white.
* **Audio:** Ambient oud and rababa soundtracks shifting into energetic stadium beats.
